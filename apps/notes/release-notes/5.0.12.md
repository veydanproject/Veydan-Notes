## New

- Notes: share text, photos and files from other apps into a new note.
- Interface: on a wide phone or a small tablet the list of notes and the open note stand side by side.
- Interface: "Report a bug" now sends the report straight from the app.

## Improved

- Interface: refreshed look with tabs, glass panels and headers.

## Fixed

- Interface: an unfolded phone (Galaxy Fold, Oppo Find) now shows two columns.
- Notes: a link to a note opens it once, and "Back" returns to where you were.
- Sync: large files now download correctly on Windows.
- Sync: a problem with the disk of one device no longer stops cleaning up old data.
- Interface: buttons and fields no longer hide under the camera cutout of the iPhone.
